Open Curtain Pathogen Vectors

EXPOSURE RISKS

Lowering dwarf wall curtains for ventilation removes heat and ammonia but opens direct biological vectors into the broiler chamber if unmanaged.

Wild Avian Vectors
Migratory birds carrying Avian Influenza (H5N1) or Salmonella through airborne droppings.
Insect & Rodent Pests
Flies, mosquitoes, and rats entering directly, acting as biological reservoirs for Gumboro & Newcastle disease.
Windborne Spores & Dust
Drafts blowing neighboring farm dust and fungal spores (Aspergillus) into the poultry bedding.

Zero-Mortality Defensive Barriers

PROTECTIVE PROTOCOLS

The zero-mortality model pairs automatic dwarf wall adjustments with static physical and chemical biosecurity barriers.

High-Density Mesh Barrier
Small-gauge steel mesh permanently mounted behind curtains stays locked even when curtains open 100%.
Extended Roof Overhangs
Deep eaves prevent wild bird droppings and rain runoff from blowing into the lower dwarf openings.
Perimeter & Water Defense
1–3m gravel rodent aprons around walls combined with continuous water-line sanitation.
